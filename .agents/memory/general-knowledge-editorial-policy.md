---
name: Quiz category editorial policy
description: Keep each mode within its factual or reasoning domain and source dated current affairs.
---

Keep General Knowledge focused on authentic world history, geography, basic science, literature, arts, and global monuments. Do not pad its pool with generated arithmetic, shopping word problems, or unrelated trivia.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a dedicated, pure GK mode rather than a mixed quantitative-question mode.

**How to apply:** Expand GK with reviewed, human-authored factual records translated into all five supported languages. Prefer factual quality over inflated question counts.

Keep every other mode isolated too: Science & Technology covers physics, space, technology, and AI; Pop Culture & Movies covers film, music, gaming, and entertainment; History & Geography covers those factual disciplines; IQ Check stays logic and reasoning. Do not route generic generated trivia into factual modes.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires category-specific questions and identifies mixed subject pools as incorrect behavior.

**How to apply:** Tag reviewed prompts by subject and validate that each category's active pool contains only its authored, approved topics. Keep procedural question generation limited to reasoning modes.

Treat current-affairs content as a dated editorial snapshot, not a live news feed. Verify additions against primary sources and distinguish scheduled events from completed events. Do not automatically relabel old facts as a new year's current affairs.

**Why:** The user requested up-to-date 2026 facts; invented achievements and future events presented as completed would undermine the educational content.

**How to apply:** Retain source evidence and a review cutoff for time-sensitive records. Recheck announced schedules when updating an edition, and disclose finite-bank reuse rather than promising unique questions for every level or player.
